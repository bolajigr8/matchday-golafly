Anatomy

1
Header is chrome, not data. --muted ground with the mono eyebrow at 9.5px. It is sticky top-0 under the content header, so it survives a 156-row scroll.
2
44px rows, borders not stripes. Zebra striping competes with the status pills for attention; a hairline does the same job silently.
3
The identity cell is two lines. Name at 600 weight, secondary in muted, both truncating with ellipsis — see Marco Silva's address. It appears on Players, Partner Apps, Transactions and both Applications tabs.
4
Dates and money are mono and tabular, right-aligned when numeric. A ragged currency column is the fastest way to make a dashboard feel untrustworthy.
5
Actions are icon-only and right-aligned, ghost until row hover — but always present in the DOM and tab order. Destructive goes last and only turns red on its own hover.
6
Selection tints with the brand at 12%, not a saturated fill. Selected rows keep their status pills readable.

Sorting is a header affordance, not a menu. One sorted column at a time, marked with a direction arrow. The existing CustomTable takes pre-rendered ReactNode cells with a headerKeyMap; that API survives this design unchanged — only the styling moves.
