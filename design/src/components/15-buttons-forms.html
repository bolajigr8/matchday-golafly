The lime pill is the only thing in the product that glows. Everything with shadow-[0_0_12px_var(--brand-glow)] is clickable, and nothing clickable glows unless it is the primary action — so the glow stays a signal rather than decoration.

In light mode the pill carries its 1px --brand-edge; in dark that token resolves to transparent and the fill separates on its own.

Required is marked on the label with a red asterisk, not by the absence of “(optional)”. Errors sit below the field with an icon, so the message is never colour-only and never pushes the layout when it appears — the help-text slot is the same line.

Checkbox and switch are not interchangeable: a checkbox is a value you are submitting, a switch is a state that takes effect immediately.
