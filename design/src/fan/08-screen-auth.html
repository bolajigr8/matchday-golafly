The API's own message is shown, not a generic one. “These credentials don’t match our records” tells a fan to check their password; “Something went wrong” sends them to support.

The button reports its own state and stays disabled while in flight, so a slow connection cannot produce two registrations.

After a successful sign-in the fan lands on the dashboard, not the marketing home. That is the whole of scenario 1, and it is a one-line redirect — but only once a session exists to redirect with.

Cookie name: golafly_fan_token, underscores only. The travels app uses golafly:token, and that colon would break this app silently: js-cookie percent-encodes the cookie name on write, Next's cookie API does not on read, so the route guard would never find a session it had just set. The admin side already documents this trap in proxy.ts.
