Fan · States

The five states every fan list has

Ported from the admin's QueryTable, adapted from table rows to cards. One wrapper — components/fan/query-state.tsx — so no screen reimplements them and none of them can be forgotten. The rules matter more here than in the admin: a fan checking a booking on a train with two bars of signal is exactly who gets punished by a layout that collapses on every refetch.

1 · Loading

Skeleton at the real card height, with varying line widths so it reads as content arriving rather than a stalled bar. Nothing shifts when the data lands.

2 · Error

{{icon:triangle-alert:22}}

Couldn’t load your bookings

Check your connection and try again. If it keeps happening, quote the reference below to support.

GET /v1/auth/ledgers → 503

Names the failing call and its status. "Something went wrong" gives the fan nothing to say to support and nothing to screenshot.

3 · Empty

{{icon:inbox:22}}

No trips booked yet

Flights, stays and eSIMs you buy will show up here with your tickets and vouchers.

Empty means nothing bought yet, so it invites — two real calls to action, not a shrug. This is scenario 4.

4 · Refetching

{{icon:plane:18}}

Flight

LOS → MAD

Confirmed
{{icon:calendar:13}} 22 Aug 2026 {{icon:ticket:13}} PNR 4K2D9X
{{icon:refresh-cw:12}} Updating

The content stays put. It never drops back to a skeleton — yanking away a booking reference someone is mid-read of, to show them a shimmer, is the worst thing this dashboard could do. The pill is the only thing that changes.

5 · No endpoint

{{icon:ticket:22}}

Match tickets aren’t connected yet

Your tickets will appear here as soon as the ticketing service is live. Nothing you bought is lost — we just can’t read it back yet.

Needs ledger_type: "ticket" on GET /v1/auth/ledgers

Deliberately not the empty state. "You have no tickets" and "we cannot see your tickets yet" are different claims, and only one of them is the fan's problem. Dashed warn border, so it never gets mistaken for a normal empty list at a glance. The endpoint is named for whoever has to fix it — see design/tickets-endpoint.md.

Never a zero where a number is unknown. Counts, prices and totals render an em-dash until they are actually measured. €0 is a claim that something cost nothing; 0 tickets is a claim that a fan owns none. Both are worse than admitting the app hasn't been told.

The filtered-empty state is intentionally absent from the shared wrapper — it has to echo back the active filters, so it belongs to whichever screen owns them. Same call as the admin.
