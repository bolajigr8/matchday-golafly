Brand

The lime is the whole identity

One accent, used for actions and active states only. It stays bright in both themes and always carries near-black text — the app never inverts to white-on-lime. --primary and --sidebar-primary are both aliases of --brand, so retinting the product is a one-line change.

Fill and type are different jobs. The lime fill is identical in both themes; lime as type is not, and cannot be — it measures 1.16:1 on a light page. That is what --brand-text is for, and it is the token that was missing. See the Brand — one lime, both themes card for the full audit.
