Rules

01
Fixed order, never cycled. Colour follows the entity, not its rank — filtering a series out must never repaint the survivors. A 6th series folds into “Other”.
02
Cap 5 adjacent, cap 3 all-pairs. Bars, stacks, donuts and funnels compare neighbours, so 5 slots hold. Scatter and bubble compare everything to everything — only slots 1–3 clear that. Past three, facet.
03
The brand lime is never a series. It means “you can click this”. A chart that borrows it competes with every CTA on the page. --chart-1 is the brand-adjacent green that carries the resemblance instead.
04
Text wears text tokens. Values, axis labels and legend text stay --foreground / --muted-foreground. The coloured mark beside the label carries identity; the label itself never does.
05
Never two y-axes. Two measures of different scale become two charts, small multiples, or both indexed to a common base.
06
Identity is never colour alone. ≥2 series always get a legend; ≤4 also get direct labels. Every chart has a table view behind it.

Not interchangeable

Status is a separate palette

Status encodes a state a row is in. Series colour encodes which thing a mark is. Reusing one for the other is how a dashboard starts lying — a green bar that means “series 1” next to a green pill that means “approved”.
