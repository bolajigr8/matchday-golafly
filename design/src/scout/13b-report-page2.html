Scout summary

A genuinely quick, direct winger who is a problem for any full-back one-on-one. Comfortable off either flank, though everything good happens on his right. At 20 he is closer to ready than most in this camp.

Key strengths

Acceleration over ten yards. 1v1 in tight space. Ball control at speed — he does not need to slow down to take a touch.

Areas for improvement

Left foot is avoidable, and defenders have worked it out. Defensive work rate is well below what a senior side would accept.

Playing style

Touchline winger. Wants the ball to feet, facing up, with grass in front of him.

Potential

Second-tier European starter is realistic if the weak foot comes. Ceiling is higher if the defensive side arrives with it.
