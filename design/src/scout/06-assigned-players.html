Two columns are missing on purpose. There is no select checkbox: every bulk action a scout could want here — assign, reassign, unassign — belongs to the admin, and a checkbox column implies otherwise. And there is no delete: a scout cannot remove a player from their own list, so the row menu carries view, evaluate, message and download only. The edit pencil dims once a report is submitted, matching the read-only lock in the States card.
