Overview — before the ticket endpoint lands

Camps and travel are live on day one: GET /v1/transactions and GET /v1/overview both already exist, and transactions is unpaginated with a created_at on every row, so the period buckets can be summed client-side with no new endpoint. Match tickets has no backend at all — features/tickets/api.ts is a deliberate mock and store/matchday.ts is static data. This card is what the Overview actually shows until that is fixed.

Em-dashes, never zeroes. €0 and 0 orders are a claim that nothing sold. That is a completely different — and much worse — statement than "this is not wired up yet", and it is the kind of number that ends up in a board deck. The warn pill names the gap, and the empty state names the endpoint that closes it, so nobody has to go and ask.

The ticket rows in Needs attention and the whole Next fixtures band are hidden on day one for the same reason, rather than shown empty. An action queue that lists work you cannot see, and a fixture table with no sell-through, are both worse than their own absence.
